An Ode to Winter

Winter, above any other season, is my favorite time of year.

From the cold weather, the hibernating animals, long nights, and cozy clothing,

I wish winter was longer than only a few months.

My Favorite Parts of Winter

Opposite of summer, winter has long quiet nights of solitude.
The landscape covered in a blanket of snow bring new beauty to the land.
Animal activity winds down as many of them hibernate through the winter.
The hallmark of the season is Christmas and the Winter solistice.
Whose woods these are I think I know.
His house is in the village though;
He will not see me stopping here
To watch his woods fill up with snow.
-Robert Frost
